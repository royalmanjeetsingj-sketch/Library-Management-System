## About

Study Space is a management platform for self-study centers, libraries and reading rooms. It replaces paper registers and scattered spreadsheets with one organised system, so owners spend less time on paperwork and more time running their space.

The official website is **[Study.mrharshit.in](https://Study.mrharshit.in)**. The Android app gives you the same experience in an installable form.

> This repository is the **official public page** for Study Space: announcements, app downloads and installation help. Study Space is a closed, private product, so the source code is not published here.

## What you can do with Study Space

| Area | What it helps with |

| **Students** | Keep every student's details and records in one place |
| **Seats** | See which seats are taken and which are free |

| **Notifications** | Send notices and reminders to your students |

## Download the Android app

1. Go to the **[Releases page](../../releases/latest)**.
2. Under **Assets**, tap **StudySpace.apk** to download it.

The download link always points to the newest version, so you never need to look for it.

**Requirements:** an Android phone or tablet and an internet connection.

---

## How to install (Android)

1. **Download** `StudySpace.apk` from the latest release.
2. **Open the file** from your notification bar or your *Downloads* folder.
3. If Android asks, tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source** (also called *Install unknown apps*) for your browser or file manager.
4. Go back and tap **Install**.
5. If Google Play Protect shows a warning, tap **More details** and then **Install anyway**. This message appears for any app installed outside the Play Store.
6. Tap **Open** and sign in, or create your account.

You can turn *Install unknown apps* back off after installing.

---

## How to update

1. Open the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)**.
2. Download the new `StudySpace.apk`.
3. Open it and tap **Update** (or **Install**). Your existing data stays safe, because it lives in your account and not in the file.

You don't need to uninstall the old version first.

## Verify your download (optional)

The checksum of each APK is listed in its release notes. To check yours:

- **Android:** use any file-hash app and compare the SHA-256 value.
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `Get-FileHash .\StudySpace.apk -Algorithm SHA256`
- **Mac / Linux:** `sha256sum StudySpace.apk`

If the value does not match the release notes, delete the file and download it again from this page.
