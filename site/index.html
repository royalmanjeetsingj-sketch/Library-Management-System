Install the Android app

It takes about a minute. You only need to allow installs from your browser once.

  1. Download the APKTap “Download Android app” above. The file is called StudySpace.apk.
  2. Open the fileOpen it from the notification bar or your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow installation if askedTap Settings, then turn on “Allow from this source” for your browser or file manager.
  4. Tap InstallIf Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details, then Install anyway. This appears for any app installed outside the Play Store.
  5. Open and sign inTap Open, then sign in or create your account.

Update the app

Download the newest APK from this page and install it over the old one. Your data stays safe because it is stored in your account. You don't need to uninstall first.

Questions

Is this the official page?

Yes. Only download Study Space from this page or from Study.mrharshit.in. Don't install copies from other sources.

The app won't install. What should I check?

Make sure installs from your browser are allowed, you have free storage, and the download finished fully. Then try again.

Can I verify my download?

Yes. Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum. Compare it with your file using a hash app, or sha256sum StudySpace.apk on a computer.

Is the source code available?

No. Study Space is a private, closed-source product.

Where do I get help?

Open an issue on this repository or contact us through the website.
